# AgriTunisie — AI Components

## 1. Plant Disease Detection — YOLOv8

YOLOv8 is used to detect plant diseases from images of leaves. The model analyzes submitted images and identifies potential disease symptoms.

## 2. Plant Disease Classification — EfficientNetB0

EfficientNetB0 is used for image classification to help identify plant disease categories.

## 3. Intelligent Irrigation — XGBoost

XGBoost analyzes environmental and soil-related data, including:
- Soil moisture
- Temperature
- Air humidity

The model supports irrigation-related predictions and decision-making.

## 4. Agricultural Assistant — RAG

The platform includes a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant designed to provide users with relevant agricultural information and answers to their questions.

## AI Integration

These AI components are part of the AgriTunisie platform and support its goal of combining artificial intelligence, IoT monitoring, and agricultural decision support.

*Note: This repository presents the project and its technologies. Model files, datasets, and implementation code are not publicly distributed.*
